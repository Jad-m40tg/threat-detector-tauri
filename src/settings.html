      Consent is required before the AI overview can run.

      When enabled, the scanned text and the scan result are sent to the selected provider. Free tiers may use this data to improve their products. Do not use this feature with private or confidential content.

      Free keys come from console.groq.com/keys (Groq) and aistudio.google.com (Gemini). Free tiers are rate-limited and sometimes busy, so adding two providers makes the feature much more reliable.
